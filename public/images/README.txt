Dish photos go in this folder, named exactly like the menu item ids in
lib/menu.ts — for example:

  combo-platter.jpg
  chicken-shawarma-rice.jpg
  lamb-gyro-rice.jpg
  chicken-biryani.jpg
  kobideh-kabob.jpg
  chicken-nuggets-fries.jpg
  chicken-tikka-kabob.jpg
  lamb-shank.jpg
  sultani-kabob.jpg
  falafel-platter.jpg
  ... (all 36 ids are in lib/menu.ts)

Square photos around 800x800 look best. A missing photo is fine — the card
simply shows a plain background until a real photo is added.
